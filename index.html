      My RPG Maker

    You can find this by clicking the " RPG Game " button, there you will find my RPG Game that I made in class using RPG Maker MZ called Get to School 7. GTS7 is a game revolving around Reid and his sister Ela who live alone, working their way as a swordsman ever since magic powers were randomly brought to earth. Since Magic Powers were brought to earth, school moved down from being a priority so even smaller paycheck for teachers. This made all teachers quit their jobs and schools were all eventually shut down. The Goal of Reid of Ela is to defeat the one who brought the Magic Powers because his very existence is why magic powers are real in their universe.
