Anime

Top 3 Anime/Manga

  1. Attack on Titan
    Best story, entertaining throughout all seasons, unique power system, has top 5 best plot twists in anime/manga and is the complete opposite of the generic anime formula which is main character gets introduced into the powers within their world, wants to be the strongest and has a rival whom fights alongside him

  2. Code Geass
    Top 3 main characters in anime, unique power system, top 5 plot twist in anime, once again takes a long step away from the generic anime formula and starts slow but easily becomes a favorite

  3. Full Metal Alchemist Brotherhood
    One of the most unique shonen anime to this day, one of my favorite power systems, elite story, top 10 anime main character, the best anime brothers aside from the generic Sasuke and Itachi and great quotes
